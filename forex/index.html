C1 and C2 rules

A setup only shows when it passes the rules you tick. Others are hidden (tap “Show” above the list to see them).
C1 – the range that gets swept
C2 – the sweep candle
Always applied (identification, not a filter): C2 must trade through C1’s high or low (a touch is not a sweep) and close strictly inside C1. Closing exactly on C1’s edge is rejected; sweeping both sides is ambiguous and not a setup. These rules decide which candles are C1 and C2; the boxes above only hide setups, they never change C1 or C2. C3 closure setups (C2 closes outside C1, then C3 closes back through C2’s open) are shown separately and trade C4.
Grade A = proper C1 + key level + bias not against · B = proper C1 + key level or bias · C = the rest. One grade lower when C2 is not a clean sweep with a rejection close.

Formed – a closed candle (C2) swept C1’s high/low and closed back inside. The setup keeps its original C1/C2 through C3 and C4 (“Formed · C4”) until it reaches the other side of C1, goes beyond the sweep, or C4 ends; a later candle never replaces that C2.
Forming – the current candle has swept the previous high/low and is back inside now. Becomes Formed if it closes there.
Sweeping – the current candle is beyond the previous high/low, not back inside yet.
C1 – the range candle: the last candle whose high and low are still untouched. If the candles after it all stay inside its range, C1 is that earlier range candle, not the previous one (up to 10 candles back). It must be a proper candle and its sweep should happen at a key level; see the rules.
C2 – the first candle after C1 that sweeps C1’s high or low (inside bars don’t count) and closes back inside C1. T-spot = 50% of C2’s wick, where C3 should hold.
On the chart – C1 is the amber column with a box, candles inside C1 are faded (“inside ×n”), C2 is the red column and its thick red wick is the part that swept C1 (turtle soup), C3/C4 are purple. The detail list gives the date/time of C1 and C2 so you can find them on TradingView.
C3 closure – C2 swept but closed outside C1; C3 then closed back through C2’s open, so C4 is the expected continuation.
Model-1 – on the lower timeframe (Monthly→Daily, Weekly→4H, Daily→1H, 4H→15m, 1H→5m): after the sweep, the opening price of the last opposite candle (or candle series) before the sweep extreme. A close back through it is the entry; stop beyond the sweep, target the other side of the CRT range. When that candle is mostly wick, its high/low is used instead.
Anticipated – no sweep yet, price is within 25% of the previous candle's high/low.
